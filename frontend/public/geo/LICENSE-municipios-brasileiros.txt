City data for Brazilian states (frontend/public/geo/by-country/BR.json's "states" field)
is sourced from https://github.com/kelvins/municipios-brasileiros (MIT License),
which compiles official IBGE (Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatistica) data.

The original countrycitystatejson package (see LICENSE-countrycitystatejson.txt)
left several Brazilian states with empty city lists (including Sao Paulo, Rio de
Janeiro and Minas Gerais), so all 27 Brazilian states were replaced with this more
complete and accurate source instead. Every other country's data is unmodified
from countrycitystatejson.
